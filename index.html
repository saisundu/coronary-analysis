Where I Am Now

Throughout this site, Sundar refers to me — Sundaresan Krishnamurthy, the patient. I am a software engineer, 47 years old, with no medical training. In October 2026 I had an unexpected cardiac admission and stent procedure. What follows is my account of what happened — written because I believe I deserve to understand what was done to my body, and because I have questions I have not yet had answered to my satisfaction.

18 October 2026 — First symptoms

During a late breakfast in Bangalore I experienced mild burning chest discomfort. I did not connect it to anything cardiac at the time — I assumed it was digestive. I drove from Bangalore that night, departing around 11 pm, stopping twice to charge the car, arriving at approximately 3:50 am on 19 October.

19 October 2026, early morning — First clinic

On waking the same burning returned and persisted for more than 30 minutes. I drove myself to a nearby emergency clinic. An ECG was done and I received ketorolac plus two other injections — the clinic treated it as a possible gastric problem. The discomfort subsided. I want to be clear: I am stating this as a timing observation only, not as my conclusion about what caused the relief.

I have a documented allergy to ketorolac. Facial and eye swelling appeared approximately two hours after the injection. This is recorded in the Manipal Hospital discharge summary exactly as: "Allergy to KETOROLAL."

19 October 2026 — Decision to go to Manipal

My son independently checked the ECG photograph using an AI assistant. The response suggested a possible cardiac event. That was a prompt for concern — not a clinical diagnosis. The family decided together to go to Manipal Hospital Salem. I drove myself there.

19 October 2026 — Manipal Hospital: admission and procedure

After a troponin result flagged as abnormal and two further ECGs, the team advised admission for an angiogram. After the angiogram was completed — more than ten hours after admission — I was asked on the procedure table whether to proceed with a stent. I was told placing it then was preferable to delaying. I agreed. A stent was placed in the LCX artery (Xience Sky, 3.0mm × 28mm). I felt stable throughout. I am not presenting that as evidence that no cardiac event occurred — it is context for the reviewer to weigh.

What I am bringing to you

I have several questions I have not been able to resolve from the records alone:

  • Does the complete evidence satisfy the diagnostic criteria for NSTEMI?
  • Was the stent fully expanded? (No IVUS/OCT was performed)
  • Did the ketorolac reaction play any role in the cardiac findings?
  • Was same-sitting PCI the most appropriate decision given the clinical picture?
  • Is my current medication regimen optimal?

The computational analysis on the QCA Analysis page is my attempt to get an independent data point on the stenosis severity. It has significant limitations — which are documented in full.
